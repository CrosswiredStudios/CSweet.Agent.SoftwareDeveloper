# 2.2.2

A completion report is no longer discarded because of the form of a finding ID.

From Super Awesome Games ticket VGD2A04892DB-38 (2026-11-13). The Engineer spent 18 minutes on a corrective ticket,
added the root Dockerfile the delivery finding asked for, and ran every check green. The ticket was then blocked with
"The completion report findingResolutions entries each require a findingId from the prompt…".

- **Cause.** The corrective ticket named its delivery finding by GUID (`c88bb3db-8427-4c33-a5e1-07e22297593f`, 36
  characters) and carried no review feedback, so there were no `R1.2`-style prompt IDs. The model answered the finding
  under its GUID. `ImplementationOutcomeReader` rejected any `findingId` longer than 32 characters, and a rejected
  report blocks the ticket.
- **Fix.** The reader rejects only entries it cannot use: no ID, an unknown resolution, or no evidence. IDs may be up
  to 128 characters and are trimmed. Evidence longer than 4,000 characters is cut to 4,000 instead of refusing the
  report. An entry whose ID matches no review finding was already ignored by the no-change rework logic.
- The prompt now says a finding named only in the ticket text may be answered under its ID as written.

After deploying, retry the blocked ticket. The workspace keeps the finished work; the agent re-validates and writes a
new report.
