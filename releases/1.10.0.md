- Move claimed development tickets into Doing before model planning begins, while retaining restart checkpoints.
- Resolve bug fixes and improvements against existing completed projects and reuse their source repository.

- Offer task, story, and epic merge approval plus review-first; read and change saved preferences through ordinary chat.
- Retain exact-publication review checkpoints across restarts and bound review repair attempts.

Requires the matching C-Sweet platform migration and SDK 3.50.0. Approve the new task-review and merge-preference capabilities and task-review event subscription through the normal upgrade review.
Existing prepared tasks and duplicate repositories are preserved; source histories are not automatically merged.
